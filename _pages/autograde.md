## What is [Autograde](https://autograde.org)

There have been auto-grading systems that greatly benefits education, but rare of them is applicable for grading code-based assignments. Autograde was created for such use, with configurable program testing environment and integrated MOSS plagiarism detection. Autograde has played a part in more than 10 curricula at [Syracuse University](https://www.syracuse.edu/) and [University of Alabama at Birmingham](https://www.uab.edu/). For more details, please check out its [document](https://autograde.org/docs/).
